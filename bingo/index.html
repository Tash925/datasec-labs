Why I created this collection

I created the DataSec Bingo Collection as the kind of resource I wished I had when I began exploring cybersecurity and building my own career transition. Learning, projects, and career transitions can feel overwhelming when every roadmap presents a long list of tools, certifications, tasks, and job titles. These boards break big goals into smaller actions so learners can build confidence, recognize their progress, and keep moving forward. This is not a fixed curriculum—it is a growing collection that will continue to evolve as I learn, build, and climb it myself.
