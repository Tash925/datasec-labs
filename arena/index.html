Why the Arena exists

Reading introduces a concept. Practice helps it stick. DataSec Arena is designed to complement the DataSec Bingo Collection and other DataSec Labs experiences by giving learners engaging ways to reinforce what they have already explored.

Choose your level

The Arena can grow with the learner. Activities may eventually be organized by level so beginners and more experienced learners can find the right place to practice.

🟢 Foundations Arena Beginner-friendly activities focused on essential cybersecurity concepts.
🔵 Intermediate Arena More applied practice involving logs, frameworks, investigations, and analysis.
🟣 Advanced Arena Future activities for threat hunting, detection engineering, malware analysis, and more.
